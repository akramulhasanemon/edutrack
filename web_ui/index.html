EduTrack is a 100% offline-first academic management, trajectory modeling, and advising workspace designed specifically for BUBT CSE students and faculty members.

01 Dashboard: Live CGPA standing, enrolled courses marks radar, waiver goals & schedule.
02 Trajectory Planner: S1 → S6 GPA velocity curve and Dean's Honor Roll pacing.
03 CGPA Calculator: Interactive semester GPA estimator with BUBT grade point scale.

05 Analytics: Grade distribution breakdown and 142-credit completion tracker.
06 Attendance: Weekly class timetable and 75% examination sitting eligibility audit.
